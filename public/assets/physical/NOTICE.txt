Smart Lab visual reference assets

Franka Emika Panda visual meshes are derived from the publicly available
franka_description and Google DeepMind MuJoCo Menagerie description.
Menagerie revision: 98a70c569c4c1b6da467a20012f766ee1f8dc521
Source: https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/98a70c569c4c1b6da467a20012f766ee1f8dc521/franka_emika_panda
License: Apache-2.0, included in PANDA-LICENSE.txt.

Modifications by the Smart Lab project: visual submeshes were converted,
merged by link/material, indexed, quantized and meshopt-compressed into GLB;
the joint hierarchy was preserved and a display pose and PBR materials added.
No MuJoCo physics, collision geometry or real robot controls are included.
This is a visual reference, not a statement that the screenshot's robots
are Franka devices or that the model matches an actual installation.

The mobile base and laboratory equipment geometry are independent reference
designs created for Smart Lab. AI generated reference images are separately
documented in docs/design/README.md; they are not photographs of real devices.
